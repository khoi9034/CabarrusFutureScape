# CFS Investment

> **Retired historical reference:** The Investments product and CASE-1 workflow are retired and are not part of the active Demo, Local, Enterprise, acceptance, or deployment surface. This document is retained only as historical design or evidence and must not be used as an operating runbook.

CFS Investment is the private/internal CFS product for land, property, and real-estate opportunity screening. It uses evidence from CFS Planning and CFS Economics, including development-readiness signals, acquisition-basis context, comparable-sale context, 2024 ACS market-area context, WSACC sewer-proximity proxies, and environmental screening context.

The product remains behind the local convenience access gate until real authentication is implemented. The gate is not production authentication.

## Primary Workflows

- Executive Overview: candidate status, data readiness, verification needs, strategy distribution, and saved report context.
- Opportunity Engine: strategy-specific parcel screening, filters, candidate review, comparison, and Report Bucket actions.
- Candidate Intake: manual and CSV candidate intake with asking-basis analysis and review status.
- Property Research: unified parcel research context across planning, economics, market, basis, utility, and environmental evidence.
- Compare: qualitative tradeoff comparison only; no winner or purchase recommendation.
- Report Studio: deterministic structured reports for due diligence and professional review.
- Due Diligence: verification sequence and questions for planning, utilities, environmental, title, access, and comparable evidence.
- Data & Methodology: source inventory, proxy status, missing evidence, and limitations.

## Safety Language

CFS Investment is screening-level research only. It is not investment advice, not an appraisal, not a utility service or capacity confirmation, not a parcel-specific environmental determination, and not a guarantee of future value.
