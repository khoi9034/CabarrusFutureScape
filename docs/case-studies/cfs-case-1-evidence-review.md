## User Decisions Required

User approval is required for development program, developable acreage cases, density cases, units or lot calculation, finished-lot or unit value, soft-cost percentage, contingency, timeline, developer margin, and scenario acquisition basis. Professional estimates are required for horizontal cost, utility allowance, off-site improvement allowance, environmental/geotechnical conditions, access, and entitlement feasibility.
